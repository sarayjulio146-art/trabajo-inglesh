Colorimetric Technology

Color as a language for your smile.

Colorimetry is the science of measuring and interpreting color. In DentiaSense, the idea is to add chemical indicators to the paste that could change color when they interact with certain substances.

Similar to the pH strips used in laboratories, these indicators would react to changes such as acidity or the presence of specific compounds, and show a visible color as a result.

Each color would be linked to a possible oral-health signal. This is a concept for education and inspiration, not a validated medical technology.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the concept.

What is DentiaSense?

DentiaSense is a conceptual smart toothpaste brand that imagines a paste able to show colors when it touches the gums.

How does the color technology work?

In the concept, colorimetric indicators change color when they interact with certain substances, in a similar way to pH indicators.

What do the different colors mean?

Green: possible gum irritation. Yellow: possible plaque buildup. Orange: possible cavity risk. Red: possible gum inflammation. Purple: possible tartar buildup. Blue: reference color with no alert.

Is DentiaSense a real medical diagnostic tool?

No. DentiaSense is an educational concept. It does not diagnose anything. Only a dentist can evaluate your oral health.
